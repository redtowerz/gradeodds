How the score works

Each card gets a score from five signals: search interest (90-day change), price (30-day change), PSA 10 population growth, reprint risk and era. Rising attention with a flat price and slow pop growth scores highest. Attention spiking while the price has already run and pops are doubling scores lowest; that is hype you'd be buying at the top. Vintage gets a small bonus because supply can't grow.

Speculation, not financial advice. Card prices can fall fast, and a reprint announcement can halve a modern card overnight. The numbers behind each verdict live in the TRENDS list in the page script, so you can change them.
