GradeOdds privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026 · applies to the prototype at this address

What the app collects

Right now, nothing leaves your device. Card photos you upload are processed in your browser to measure centering and check photo quality; they are not sent to a server. Your collection and submission log are kept in your browser's memory for the session and are gone when you close the tab.

What the app does not do

No account, no tracking, no cookies, no advertising, no sale of data. The site loads its card catalog and images from the same place it is hosted.

When this changes

A future version will add sign-in, a saved collection, and photo analysis on a server. Before that ships, this page will be updated to say exactly what is stored, where, and for how long, and the app will ask before uploading a photo.
